Firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1

Your groovebox as a pixel-art RPG. Four tracks are four heroes, every lit step is an attack, the tempo sets how fast the party walks, the key picks the biome, effects bring the weather, and recording a loop is a boss fight. Every SLOOP function stays where it was, with the same controls.

tracksmage, archer, cleric and warrior play your four tracks
keymajor forest, minor cave, dorian coast, phrygian ruins
fxdistortion storms, chorus mist, delay echoes, reverb rain
reca wild loop appears: record it to beat the King Slime
