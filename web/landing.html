A pixel-art RPG for your FM-1

Custom firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1: the SLOOP groovebox drawn as a sidescroll adventure. Build /*VERSION*/.

InstallerPut FM1 Quest on your FM-1 over USB (Chrome or Edge) EditorBack up your FM-1, edit sounds, patterns and presets (FM-1 connected by USB) EmulatorTry the real firmware in your browser: keyboard, knobs and sound Sourcegithub.com/hericdk/fm1-quest (GPL-3.0)

FM1 Quest is based on SLOOP by isod89, which is based on Felucca by Leo Kuroshita (@kurogedelic), Hügelton Instruments. The emulator builds on sloop-web-emu by sabliran.
